## 1. Executive Summary

ChargeAtlas India provides a transparent, evidence-backed roster of **23 candidate charging locations** formulated from empirical Phase 4 GIS gap audits. These locations specifically address:
1. **Unserved Urban Cells in Pune:** High-density IT, industrial, and transit corridors lacking public EVSE access under MoP Clause 11(1)(a).
2. **Highway Deficits on Samruddhi Mahamarg:** Critical gaps along the 701 km alignment exceeding the MoP 20 km spacing mandate (Clause 11(1)(b)) and 100 km fast-charging benchmark (Clause 12(7)).
3. **Zero-Charger District Headquarters:** High-adoption urban centers with thousands of registered EVs but zero public chargers in the national BEE EV Yatra directory.

> **Legal & Engineering Disclaimer:** Candidate rankings represent **preliminary planning prioritization** based on spatial proximity, unserved fleet catchments, and publicly mapped electrical infrastructure. They do **NOT** constitute structural engineering approvals, DISCOM electricity connection sanctions, or commercial lease commitments.

## 3. Top Siting Deep Dives by Study Area

### 3.1 Pune Urban Agglomeration: Hinjawadi Phase 3 Transit Hub (`CAND-PUN-001`)
- **Coordinates:** $18.5742^\circ\text{N}, 73.6985^\circ\text{E}$
- **MCDA Score:** $89.8$ / 100 (Rank #4 State-Wide, #1 in Pune Urban)
- **Nearest Public Charger:** $4.8\text{ km}$ (Vivanta Hinjawadi)
- **Substation Proximity:** Hinjawadi Phase 1 22kV Feeder ($0.85\text{ km}$)
- **Unserved Catchment:** $14,200$ active vehicles (concentrated IT workforce E-2W and E-4W)
- **Recommendation Rationale:** Directly addresses an unserved micro-grid cell in Pune’s largest tech cluster. Aligns with MoP Clause 11(1)(a).
- **Engineering Limitations:** PMPML bus bay coordination required; MSEDCL peak feeder capacity survey pending.

### 3.2 Samruddhi Mahamarg: Igatpuri Hillways Wayside Amenity (`CAND-SAM-006`)
- **Coordinates:** $19.7020^\circ\text{N}, 73.5700^\circ\text{E}$ (Chainage 670 km)
- **MCDA Score:** $91.3$ / 100 (Rank #1 State-Wide)
- **Nearest Public Charger:** $22.0\text{ km}$
- **Substation Proximity:** Igatpuri Interchange 33kV Substation ($0.60\text{ km}$)
- **Unserved Catchment:** $21,000$ intercity vehicles
- **Recommendation Rationale:** Serves as the critical pre-ghat battery top-up hub before the steep Kasara Ghat descent into Mumbai MMR. High dwell times at existing MSRDC wayside food courts.
- **Engineering Limitations:** Heavy weekend tourist surge demand requires dynamic load-balancing and $\ge 2 \times 240\text{ kW}$ CCS-2 dispensers under Clause 12(7).

### 3.3 Zero-Charger District HQ: Nashik Central CBS Bus Terminal (`CAND-DST-001`)
- **Coordinates:** $19.9975^\circ\text{N}, 73.7898^\circ\text{E}$
- **MCDA Score:** $90.7$ / 100 (Rank #2 State-Wide)
- **Nearest Public Charger:** $16.5\text{ km}$
- **Substation Proximity:** Sinnar Industrial / City 33kV Feeder ($1.50\text{ km}$)
- **Unserved Catchment:** $48,385$ registered EVs in Nashik district
- **Recommendation Rationale:** Nashik is Maharashtra's 5th largest EV adoption market, yet currently possesses zero verified public charging stations in the BEE directory.
- **Engineering Limitations:** MSRTC depot operational clearance required; city feeder peak load survey needed.

## 4. MCLP Optimization Results (Pune Urban Case Study)

When running the Maximum Covering Location Problem solver on Pune Urban with a budget of $p = 5$ stations and a service radius of $R_c = 3.0\text{ km}$:
- **Selected Optimal Siting Network:**
  1. `CAND-PUN-001` (Hinjawadi Phase 3 Transit Hub)
  2. `CAND-PUN-004` (Chakan MIDC Phase 2 Terminal)
  3. `CAND-PUN-007` (Hadapsar Gadital PMPML Depot)
  4. `CAND-PUN-002` (Wagholi Highway Junction)
  5. `CAND-PUN-005` (Katraj Wonder City Bus Terminus)
- **Demand Coverage Achieved:** **$71.7\%$** of all unserved urban EV catchments ($59,400$ vehicles covered).
- **Geometric Distribution:** Provides balanced north-south and east-west geographic dispersion, preventing wasteful charger clustering while maximizing public access.
